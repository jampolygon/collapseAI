# Charging a phone without grid power
category: Power
source: CollapseAI Essentials

Save battery first: airplane mode, lowest screen brightness, battery saver on, close apps. Turn on data or radio only when needed.

Options:
- Solar panel: a 10 W or larger USB solar panel in direct sun can charge a phone in a few hours. Charge a power bank from the panel, then charge the phone from the power bank; this is more reliable when clouds pass. Keep the phone in the shade; heat damages batteries.
- Car battery: a 12 V car battery with a USB car charger (cigarette lighter adapter) or a 12 V-to-USB converter can charge phones many times. Do not run the car engine in a closed space.
- Motorcycle battery: same as car, using a 12 V USB adapter.
- Hand-crank radio/charger: slow, but works anywhere.

# Simple water filter (bottle filter)
category: Water engineering
source: CollapseAI Essentials

This filter removes dirt and makes water clearer. It does NOT kill germs. Always boil or disinfect the water afterwards.

1. Cut the bottom off a plastic bottle and turn it upside down (cap down, cap loosened slightly or pierced).
2. Layer from the bottom (cap end) up: a clean cloth, then crushed charcoal from a fire (not briquettes), then fine sand, then coarse sand, then small gravel.
3. Pour water in slowly at the top. Throw away the first liter that comes out.
4. Replace the sand and charcoal when the flow gets slow or the water stops getting clear.

# 12-volt power basics
category: Power
source: CollapseAI Essentials

- Car and motorcycle batteries are 12 V. LED lights, phone chargers (with adapter) and small fans for 12 V can run directly from them.
- Never short the terminals (+ and −) with metal; it can cause burns, fire or explosion.
- Connect red to + (positive) and black to − (negative).
- Use fuses close to the battery on any wire you add.
- A solar charge controller is needed between a solar panel and a battery to prevent overcharging.
- Lead-acid batteries give off hydrogen gas while charging; charge in a ventilated place, away from flames.
- To estimate how long a battery lasts: hours ≈ (battery Ah × 12 V × 0.5) ÷ device watts. The 0.5 is because lead-acid batteries should not be drained below half.

# Emergency radio and communication
category: Communication
source: CollapseAI Essentials

- A battery or hand-crank AM/FM radio receives news and warnings when phone networks are down. AM stations reach farther, especially at night.
- Text messages (SMS) often get through when calls fail. Keep messages short.
- Set your phone to 2G/3G only if 4G/5G is unstable; it uses less battery and may connect better.
- Agree on fixed times to turn on radios or phones (for example, at the top of each hour) to save battery.
- Two-way radios (walkie-talkies) work without towers over short distances; higher ground increases range.
- Write important numbers and addresses on paper.
